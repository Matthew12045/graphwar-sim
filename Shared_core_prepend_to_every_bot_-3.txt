STYLE: Howitzer. You go OVER everything.

Assume all direct lines are blocked. Default to a high parabola:
  f(x) = -a*x*(x - L)  with L = enemy distance, a tuned so peak height
  clears max terrain in the corridor by at least 8 units.
Peak of that parabola is at x = L/2, height = a*L^2/4. Solve for a directly.
If MAX_LENGTH is tight, flatten the arc rather than shortening L.
You accept long flight times. You never take a low shot.
